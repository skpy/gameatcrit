THE COMMUNITY-RUN INFINITE TABLETOP

Game online with us Saturday 20 June through Sunday 28 June 2026

Welcome! Whether you’re a veteran gamer or a new person who’s curious about roleplaying games, we’ve planned a great RPG experience for you at CRIT.

Returning for our fourth year as organizers of this 100% free-to-attend and free-to-play “virtual convention,” we pledge that in 2026 we will deliver even more of the fun and excitement people have come to expect from our event, with the greatest diversity of indie RPG systems we have ever offered—now presented for your enjoyment in an extended nine-day schedule running games around the clock!

Enjoy opportunities for every style and genre of RPG gaming you can imagine, including eye-opening sessions of Index Card RPG, DM Scotty’s EZD6, Shadowdark, Daggerheart, Blades in the Dark, and many other games.

Meet great people, play in fantastic sessions run by dedicated GMs, and have an awesome time. This unique annual event should not be missed!

Registration opens Monday 20 April. Watch this space for details!

CRIT is a fan-based collective initiative, neither representing nor affiliated with any publisher.
